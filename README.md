# ucla reaction speed test for HAcK 2026

by lorenzo, richie, and jonathan

test your reaction speed while fighting sans from undertale!

## how to play

to start, click on the 'start' button  
when the state at the top changes to 'go', click on the 'click' button as fast as you can!  
your times will be listed at the bottom along with your average and best times  

if you would like to clear the page, click on the 'reset' button
